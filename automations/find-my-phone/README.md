# Find My Phone Automation

### Change the ringtone on your phone for the chanel you used int his automation "find_my_phone"
to change the channel ringtone, go to the HA companion app > Settings > Companion app > scroll down to Notifications > Notification channels 
and chose the channel you created.  Edit it and change the sound you want to use.

I use Zedge to download ringtones to my local phone
